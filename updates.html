First engagement: India GCC go-to-market

PreeSeq signed its first engagement in its first month. We advise the India leadership of a NYSE-listed technology services company on its go-to-market for Global Capability Centres (GCCs) in India.

The work covers four areas:

  • GCC go-to-market strategy and account prioritisation
  • Offering design and sales enablement
  • Support on active pursuits
  • Partner and ecosystem development in India

The client is not named, in line with our confidentiality obligations.
