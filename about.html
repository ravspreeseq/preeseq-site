Ravi Gupta

Consultant, PreeSeq · Bengaluru, India

Ravi Gupta leads PreeSeq, advising technology and services companies and PE investors on growth - capability build, growth accelerators, large deals, inorganic growth and AI value realisation. He also takes on fractional CGO, CRO and CBO roles. Ravi has 25 years of experience in IT services across sales, solutions, delivery and consulting, serving clients in the US, Europe, APAC, India and the Middle East.

Before PreeSeq, Ravi was Senior Vice President at CitiusTech, a PE-backed healthcare technology firm, where he owned an executive portfolio covering CEO and Board value-creation planning, large deals, a practice turnaround, acquisition integration and new offerings. Earlier he spent ten years at Wipro Healthcare & Life Sciences in five leadership roles, including Global Head of Healthcare and Life Sciences Consulting and APJME Healthcare Business Head, and eight years at Genpact / GE Capital across sales, solutioning, delivery and account leadership. He began his career at Citibank and Oracle. He holds an MBA from IIM Mumbai.
