Technology services for GCCs

Scale your AI initiatives.

Everforth takes AI use cases from pilot into production for GCCs, and gives AI platform companies the capacity to deploy at their clients.

Who buys

GCC heads, sponsored by the global CIO or CDAO; process owners for agent work; leadership of AI platform companies

Industry examples

Healthcare payers, financial services, manufacturing, utilities; AI model and SaaS platform companies

A · Client priorities

GCCs and AI platform companies need different things from AI.

GCCs want AI pilots running in production. AI platform companies want capacity to deploy their platforms at client sites.

GCCs taking AI pilots into production

What it takes for each use case:

  • Production data feeds in place of pilot extracts
  • Evaluation sets built on real data
  • Builds hardened to hold up on live volumes
  • Monitoring once the use case is live
  • Security and governance sign-off
  • Deployment on the client's platform and cloud
  • A client product owner and a pod that co-builds from day one
AI platform companies that need deployment capacity

What holds deployment back:

  • Implementation demand ahead of in-house and partner capacity
  • High onshore delivery cost compressing services margins
  • Scarce talent with both domain and platform skills
  • Uneven quality and time-to-value across delivery partners
  • Limited focus on adoption after go-live
