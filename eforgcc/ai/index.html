Scaling AI

Scale your AI initiatives.

Two models on one pod capability: a production pod that takes GCC use cases live, and a deployment hub for AI platform companies.

Built for

GCCs and AI platform companies in healthcare, financial services, manufacturing and utilities

Challenges

GCCs and AI platform companies need different things from AI.

GCCs want AI pilots running in production. AI platform companies want capacity to deploy their platforms at client sites.

Model 1 · Production pod for GCCs

AI pilots that have not reached production

What it takes, per use case

  • Production data feeds in place of pilot extracts
  • Evaluation sets built on real data
  • Monitoring once the use case is live
  • Security and governance sign-off
  • Run on the client's platform and cloud
  • A client product owner and a pod that co-builds
Model 2 · Deployment hub for AI platform companies

Capacity to deploy the platform at its own clients

What holds deployment back

  • Implementation demand ahead of in-house and partner capacity
  • Scarce talent with both domain and platform skills
  • Uneven quality and time-to-value across delivery partners
  • Limited focus on adoption after go-live
