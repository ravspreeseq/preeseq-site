Approach

The commitments behind the offering.

01

Efficiency-linked delivery

Efficiency baselines, productivity targets and cost-to-serve KPIs are set upfront, then tracked through ramp-up and steady state.

02

Transfer transparency

Transfer scope, readiness criteria and commercial principles are agreed at the start, with a planned move from shadowing to independent ownership.

03

Industry and partner-led model

Industry-specific GCC playbooks and talent strategies, with strategic advisory and specialist partners for design and execution.

04

Right-sized entry

A right-sized first engagement around one priority technology capability, with team ramp-up tied to demand and room to add capabilities later.
